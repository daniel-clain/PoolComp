# Philosophy

Pool comp managers already run their competitions on paper. It works. They won't abandon it for an app — and they shouldn't have to.

## Core Constraint

The app enhances the paper system. It never replaces it.

- Paper remains the source of truth during live competitions.
- Every feature must provide value even if it's the only feature adopted.
- If the app stops working, the paper system continues unaffected.
- No feature forces a workflow change on the user.

## Progressive Adoption

The app offers a gradual path, not a forced migration:

1. **Helper mode** — individual tools that assist specific manual tasks.
2. **Hybrid mode** — the app maintains a digital mirror that you keep aligned with paper by **manual entry** (who played, who won, totals).
3. **Full digital mode** — the app becomes the primary interface, paper as backup.

Users choose how far along this path they go. Most will stay in helper mode for a long time, and that's fine.
