# How running pool comp works

- pool comp is once a week on thursday
- players must register to play before 7:00pm and pay $10 to enter
- at 7:00 the people running the comp determine all the first round random matchups and start the comp
- each matchup starts by the comp manager flipping a coin and whoever wins the coin toss chooses who breaks
- when a game finishes the players of the matchup report who won to the manager who enters the result in the tournament
- winners of each game move on to the next bracket in the tournament and rounds of the tournament progress until the finals
- the winner of the finals matchup gets all the winner's prize money and the player who comes 2nd gets 2 $40 dining vouchers

# How the first place prize money is calculated

- description: the total buyin money divided by 2, half the amount goes to the first place prize money and the other goes to the Monthy big comp minus $20 which goes to the yearly xmas event, then $50 is added to the first place prize money from the bar.
- the formula for the prize money: (BuyIn \* Players / 2) + BarInput
- for example sixteen players enter the: if BuyIn is $10 and there are 16 People and BarInput is $50 then ($10*16/2)+$50 = $130, half of the buying goes to the bar therefor 10*16/2 = $80 to the bar. in this example the bar make a $30 profit: +$80 - $50 = $30

# Monthly big comp and second chance tournament

- once a month, every 3rd thursday of the month is a special pool comp, where players who lose in the 1st round of the main tournament get a 2nd chance by being automatically entered in the 2nd tournament
  - on the day of the big comp,
    - its the regular $10 buyin: half goes to this week's normal first prize (+ $50 from the bar), and the other half minus $20 for Christmas is set aside for the **next** big comp, as usual
    - the prize pool is this week's normal first prize plus the money set aside from the last big comp and every normal comp held since then (not a fixed 4 weeks — months can contribute four or five comps)
    - the prize money for the main tournament is 70% of the total, and the prize money for the 2nd chance tournament is 30% of the total
    - in the main tournament, of the 70% prize money, the prize money for 1st and 2nd place is split again by 70% for first and 30% for 2nd
    - in the 2nd chance tournament, the first place gets the whole 30%, and the 2nd place gets 2 $20 dining vouchers
    - if a player loses in the first round of the main tournament, but they have come first within the last 6 weeks, they are not eligible to enter the 2nd chance tournament
